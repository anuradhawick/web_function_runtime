## Give each page its own metadata

A useful page title tells readers what a page contains. A description gives a short
summary that search engines may use in search results.

This page gets its title and description from the metadata at the top of its Markdown file.

## Make content easy to understand

Use one main heading, clear section headings, and descriptive links. Server-rendered
HTML makes the article available in the initial response, including when JavaScript is disabled.

These basics help make content accessible to readers and crawlers. They do not guarantee search rankings.

Read [Blog 1](/blog/blog-1) for an introduction to server-side rendering.
